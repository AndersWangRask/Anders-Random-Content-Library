# Humanoid Robot Manufacturers — State of Play, May 2026

## Why this document exists

In March 2024 I made a set of [Long Bet predictions about humanoid robots](Long%20Bet%20Predictions%20-%20Technology.md). At the time those numbers seemed optimistic. Two years on, reality is running well ahead of the early years of my forecast. This document is a snapshot of who is actually building humanoid robots, how many they expect to ship in 2026, and what they say they'll be doing by 2030 — so I can mark my predictions to market.

A note on credibility: these numbers are a mixture of (a) hard, company-confirmed factory capacity and signed orders, (b) CEO aspirations on slide decks, and (c) third-party analyst forecasts. I've tried to label which is which. The Chinese makers in particular have far more concrete near-term shipment numbers than is generally appreciated in Western coverage; the Western players tend to publish more aspirational long-horizon targets.

## The roll-up

**2025 actual industry shipments (Omdia):** ~13,318 units. Six of the top seven shippers were Chinese.

**Sum of credibly-stated 2026 capacity / targets** (company-confirmed factory capacity or signed orders, ignoring Tesla because no number was given on the Q1 2026 call, and ignoring "pilot only" companies):

AgiBot ~20–30k, Unitree 20k, BYD 20k, UBTech 5k, 1X 10k, Figure ~12k capacity, Kepler "thousands," Dobot multi-batch, Leju ~10k partner-line, Boston Dynamics low thousands committed, Agility "low thousands."

→ **Aggregate 2026 capacity: ~110,000–140,000 units**, of which ~80–90% Chinese.

**Analyst counterpoint:** Goldman Sachs base case for 2026 industry shipments is 50,000–100,000. TrendForce forecasts ~94% YoY growth (~26,000 implied). So *capacity to build* is comfortably above what analysts expect to *actually ship* — the constraint shifts from manufacturing to demand-validation and reliability.

**Sum of stated 2030-ish targets** (company-stated, treat the big numbers as aspirational):

Tesla 1M/yr by 2028–2029 (later 10M/yr aspirational), XPENG 1M units by 2030, Hyundai/Boston Dynamics 30k/yr by 2028, 1X 100k+/yr by end-2027, Figure 100k cumulative by ~2029, UBTech 10k/yr by 2027, NEURA "millions" by 2030.

→ **If you take those headline numbers at face value, aggregate 2030 capacity is well above 2.5M/yr.** Analyst forecasts are dramatically lower: Goldman base case 250k/yr in 2030, Omdia 2.6M/yr by 2035, Morgan Stanley 24.4M cumulative by 2036.

## Marking my predictions to market

Here is the side-by-side. Reality has overtaken the early years of my forecast by a wide margin; the back end is harder to call.

| My prediction (March 2024) | Reality / latest known | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **2026:** ≥300 units doing meaningful work | 2025 alone shipped ~13,318 units (Omdia). 2026 capacity is 110k+ | Wildly conservative. Beaten by ~40–100× a year early. |
| **2028:** ≥10,000 units cumulative | Already exceeded in 2025. AgiBot+Unitree alone shipped >10k that year | Beaten three years early. |
| **2029:** ≥100,000/year | Plausible to be hit in 2026 or 2027, depending on demand | Likely beaten 2–3 years early. |
| **2030:** ≥250,000/year (cumulative ≥360,000) | Goldman base case for 2030 is exactly ~250k/year; aggregate company targets sum to multiples of that | Tracking — close to the analyst base case, well below the bullish company aggregates. |
| **2032:** ≥10M/year, cumulative ~11M | Morgan Stanley cumulative 24.4M by 2036 implies ~10M/yr in the 2032–2034 range under bullish scenarios | Roughly on track in bullish case; conservative analyst base cases say later. |
| **2050:** 2.5B–12B cumulative in use | No useful 25-year forecast yet; depends entirely on whether current curve holds | Too early to call — but the early ramp is consistent with the bullish end of this range. |

The pattern is interesting: the steep early ramp got steeper than I expected, but the analyst consensus is still that the inflection to "tens of millions a year" lands in the early-to-mid 2030s — not radically earlier than I had it. So my 2032 number of 10M/yr is, surprisingly, still close to the bullish analyst case rather than miles too low.

The thing I most clearly underestimated was the Chinese manufacturing curve. AgiBot going from prototype to 10,000 cumulative units in roughly a year, and Unitree guiding to 20,000 in a single year, are 2025–2026 facts that I would not have predicted in March 2024.

## Five things I didn't expect

1. **China owns the unit volume, not just the factories.** Six of the top seven 2025 shippers were Chinese; AgiBot + Unitree are forecast at ~80% of 2026 shipments. Western analyst narratives still centre Tesla / Figure / 1X but they shipped a small fraction of what AgiBot did.
2. **Tesla is meaningfully behind its own promises.** Musk admitted in January 2026 that "none are doing useful work" yet, the 5,000-unit 2025 target produced "a few hundred", and on the Q1 2026 call he refused to give a 2026 number — a sharp climbdown from the 50–100k chatter of 12–18 months ago.
3. **1X sold 10,000 NEO consumer pre-orders in five days at $20,000/unit.** The first real demand-side signal from the home-robot category, and it materially predates most industry expectations of consumer-ready humanoids.
4. **Boston Dynamics is fully committed for 2026 to just two customers** (Hyundai + Google DeepMind). The most-recognised Western humanoid brand is effectively unavailable on the merchant market this year.
5. **The price floor is collapsing fast.** Dobot Atom at $27,500, Unitree G1 from $16k, Kepler K2 from $30k — Chinese players are already selling industrial-grade humanoids below Goldman's 2026 cost-curve estimate ($15k–$20k BOM). The Western entry point is converging fast.

Bonus: **Mobileye paid $900M for Mentee Robotics** — first big M&A in the humanoid pure-play space, signalling that auto-AI incumbents now see physical-AI / humanoid stacks as core.

## What I'd update in my predictions

If I were writing the Long Bet today, I would:

- Pull the 2026, 2028, 2029 numbers forward by 2–3 years each (they've all been beaten early).
- Hold the 2030 number around ~500k–1M/yr — analyst base case says ~250k, bullish company aggregates say 2M+; the truth is probably in between.
- Hold 2032 at roughly 10M/yr — surprisingly close to where the bullish analyst cases land.
- Add a separate prediction: **majority of 2026–2028 production will be Chinese**, which is now obviously true but was not at all obvious in March 2024.
- Add: **first $10k consumer humanoid shipping in volume by 2028.** The 1X NEO pre-orders and Chinese price points make this a more aggressive but defensible call.
